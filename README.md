## English

Atelier Pizza works out everything you need for great homemade pizza:

- **Simple mode**: answer 4 questions (how many pizzas, when to eat, your oven, your toppings) and get only what you need, your schedule and baking. **Full mode** shows every setting.
- dough, sauce and topping amounts for the number and size of your pizzas;
- a step-by-step schedule, starting from the time you want to eat;
- several methods: poolish, biga, express, long straight, cold ferment, sourdough, pan pizza, focaccia, gluten-free;
- baking tailored to your oven (stone, steel, sheet pan);
- cost, calories, shopping list and timers;
- a recipe book you can save and share.

**On a phone:** open the link, then “Add to Home Screen” (Safari on iPhone) or “Install app” (Chrome on Android). It opens full screen with its own icon and works offline once opened.

**Languages:** opens in your device’s language (French, English, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese or Chinese).

**Privacy:** no account, no data sent. Your recipes stay in your browser.

## Português

O Atelier Pizza calcula tudo o que você precisa para fazer uma boa pizza em casa:

- **Modo simples**: 4 perguntas (quantas pizzas, quando comer, seu forno, sua cobertura) e só o essencial: do que você precisa, seu cronograma e o cozimento. O **modo completo** mostra todos os ajustes.

**No celular:** abra o link e depois “Adicionar à Tela de Início” (Safari no iPhone) ou “Instalar app” (Chrome no Android). Ele abre em tela cheia com o próprio ícone e funciona sem internet depois de aberto uma vez.

## 中文

Atelier Pizza 帮你算好在家做出美味披萨所需的一切：

- **简易模式**：回答 4 个问题（做几张披萨、什么时候吃、你的烤箱、你的配料），只显示必要内容：所需材料、时间表和烘烤方法。**完整模式**显示所有设置。
- 按披萨数量和尺寸计算面团、酱和配料的用量；
- 从用餐时间倒推出逐步的时间表；
- 多种方法：波兰种、意式酵头（biga）、快速法、长时间直接法、冷藏发酵、天然酵种、烤盘披萨、佛卡夏、无麸质；
- 根据你的烤箱安排烘烤（披萨石、烤钢板、烤盘）；
- 成本、卡路里、购物清单和计时器；
- 可保存和分享的配方本。

**在手机上：**打开链接，然后选择“添加到主屏幕”（iPhone 上的 Safari）或“安装应用”（Android 上的 Chrome）。它会带着自己的图标全屏打开，首次打开后即可离线使用。

**语言：**以设备的语言打开（法语、英语、西班牙语、意大利语、葡萄牙语或中文）。

**隐私：**无需账号，不发送任何数据。你的配方只保存在你的浏览器中。
